# GuestWall

> Your website. Your design. Your guestbook.

GuestWall is an open-source guestbook platform for your personal websites, portfolios, blogs, & more.

It gives you a place where guests can leave messages, say hello, or share where they're from.

## Why GuestWall?

Guestbooks used to be a small but meaningful part of the web. You'd visit someone's site, read through what other people had written, and leave a message of your own.

I want to bring that back.

GuestWall is basically the modern version of that idea. It's meant for people who have their own website and want a small place where visitors can leave a message.

It is not supposed to be another social network. It is simply a guestbook.

## How does it work?

Create your own GuestWall and share it with your visitors.

`domain.tld/your-name`

Visitors can write a message without needing an account.

You can then manage the messages from your dashboard, hide messages you don't want displayed, delete spam, or pin your favorites.

## Features

- Personal GuestWalls
- Public Messages
- No visitor accounts required
- Message moderation
- Pin Messages
- Spam Protection
- Customizable appearance
- Open Source!!
- Custom Domains

## Customization

Your GuestWall should feel like a part of your website, not like an external service.

GuestWall will support customization for things like:

- Colors
- Light & dark mode
- Fonts
- Layout
- Borders
- Custom CSS
